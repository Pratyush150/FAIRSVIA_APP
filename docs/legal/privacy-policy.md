## 1. What we collect

### From every user
| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number | Sign-up and every login | Your account identity; we text you a one-time login code |
| Name, email, profile photo (if you add them) | Your profile | Shown to the other party on a trip; receipts |
| Device push token | When you allow notifications | Trip updates on your phone |
| Saved places (e.g. home, work) | When you save one | Faster booking |
| Ratings you give and receive | After a trip | Service quality and safety |
| Support tickets and messages | When you contact support | Resolving your request |
| In-trip chat messages | During a trip | Letting rider and driver coordinate the pickup |
| Safety (SOS) reports | If you use the safety feature | Reviewed by our safety team |
| Technical data: IP address, app version, error reports | When you use the service | Security, rate limiting, fixing crashes |

### Riders
| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Pickup, drop-off and stops | To price, dispatch and complete the trip |
| Your location while booking and during a trip | Showing nearby drivers, pickup, and live trip progress |
| Trip history (routes, times, fares) | Receipts, support, disputes |
| Payment method | Charging for trips — see "Payments" below |
| Name and phone of someone you book a ride for | So the driver can find them and we can text them trip details |

### Drivers
| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Location, continuously **while you are online** | Matching you with nearby riders and showing your approach to the rider. We do not track you while you are offline |
| Vehicle make, model, colour, plate number, licence number | Shown to riders for identification; compliance |
| Background check result | Required to drive on the platform (see "Who we share with") |
| Earnings, payouts and ledger | Paying you correctly |
| Payout account (held by our payment processor) | Sending your earnings |

**Payments.** Card details are entered into and held by our payment processor
(Stripe). FAIRSVIA never receives or stores your full card number — we keep
only a reference, the card brand and the last four digits.

**Location precision.** Trip routes are stored with trip records. Live
positions are held only in short-lived memory for matching and are not kept as
a continuous history.

## 2. How we use it

- To provide the service: accounts, booking, matching, navigation, payments,
  receipts, payouts.
- For safety: identity verification, driver screening, SOS handling, ratings,
  and investigating incidents.
- To keep the service secure: fraud prevention, rate limiting, abuse detection,
  and an internal audit log of administrator actions.
- To improve reliability: error and crash reports, aggregated performance data.
- To contact you about your trips and account. We send marketing only if you
  agree, and you can opt out at any time.
- To comply with law, including tax and transport regulation, and lawful
  requests from authorities.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 3. Who we share it with

- **The other party on your trip.** Riders see the driver's name, rating,
  vehicle and plate. Drivers see the rider's name, rating, and the pickup and drop-off.
  Your own phone number is not shown to the other party. **One exception:**
  when you book a ride for someone else, the driver sees that passenger's name
  and phone number so they can find and call them — tell them before you book.
- **Service providers** acting on our instructions:
  - payment processing — Stripe
  - text messages and email — Amazon Web Services (SNS, SES)
  - push notifications — Google Firebase Cloud Messaging
  - maps, routing and place search — Google Maps Platform and/or
    self-hosted OpenStreetMap services
  - driver background checks — [BACKGROUND-CHECK PROVIDER FOR THE LAUNCH COUNTRY]
  - error monitoring — Sentry
  - hosting — [HOSTING PROVIDER AND COUNTRY]
- **Authorities**, when required by law or to protect someone's safety.
- **A successor**, if FAIRSVIA is merged or sold, under this policy.

Some of these providers process data outside your country. Where the law of
your country restricts this, we [DESCRIBE SAFEGUARD / LOCAL STORAGE — TO BE
CONFIRMED WITH COUNSEL].

## 5. Your choices and rights

Subject to the law of your country, you can:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
- **correct** inaccurate data (most of it directly in the app);
- **delete** your account and personal data;
- **object** to or restrict certain uses, and withdraw consent (e.g. for
  notifications or location — though booking and driving need location);
- **complain** to your data-protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] from the phone number
or email on your account, or use in-app support. We respond within [30] days.

## 6. Security

Data travels encrypted (TLS 1.2+). Access to production systems is restricted
to authorised staff, administrator actions are audit-logged, and databases are
backed up daily. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your
data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 7. Children

FAIRSVIA is not for people under 18. Riders must be 18 or older to hold an
account. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
