# FAIRSVIA product audit — 2026-09-25

**Scope:** rider + driver apps v2.2.0 (Plan F "Map Glass", build 6200), backend, ops, and docs.
**Method:** I used every listed screen on the headless emulator `emulator-5554` (1080×2400, 420 dpi ≈ a 411×914 dp phone) in light and dark mode, with real rides against the dev backend (idle, slow and demo fake drivers, plus two rides where the emulator driver app accepted). I also read the plans, the icon list, CLAUDE.md and the remaining-work plan, and ran some checks in code and on the command line.
**Screenshots:** `docs/brand/research/audit-2026-09-25/` (`r-*` = rider, `d-*` = driver, `*-light` / `*-dark`).
**Read-only:** I changed no code. Rider and driver were both returned to light mode, and every trip I created is in a final state (completed, cancelled or no drivers).

## (C) Top 10 fixes, ranked by impact ÷ effort

| Rank | Fix | Impact | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dark OTP phone number invisible.** Use `colorScheme.onSurface` instead of `AppColors.textPrimaryLight` (`otp_page.dart:117`), and review the other fixed colours in apps/*/lib and packages/core (14 uses of `textPrimaryLight` or `Colors.white`/`Colors.black`, plus 10 raw `Color(0x…)` outside design_system; not every one is a bug). | High (every dark-mode sign-in) | XS |
| 2 | **Remove the stray Lottie dash.** Hold the money animation on a meaningful last frame, hide it after it plays, or use a static icon. | High (every completed ride, both apps) | XS |
| 3 | **Complete-trip guard.** Refuse or confirm "Complete" when the car is more than about 300 m from the drop-off or under 50 m has been driven, and pro-rate instead of charging 100 % of the estimate. Align the "metered" copy. | High (money and fraud) | S |
| 4 | **Anchor the radar to the pickup** through the map projection, not a fixed overlay position. Re-verify in dark mode and after camera moves. | High (the first "is it working?" moment) | S |
| 5 | **Warning and banner contrast.** Use a darker warning colour for text (≥ 4.5:1) and dark-on-amber banners. Take all banners from tokens. | Med-high (accessibility and legibility) | XS |
| 6 | **En-route header.** Move Safety and ••• to their own row, or shorten the title so it never orphans "min". Add Call on trip for parity with en route. | Medium (the most-viewed live screen) | S |
| 7 | **One formatter for plates and phones.** Spaced plates and "+91 90000 00001" everywhere, including share text, Ride details, OTP and the input mask. | Medium (trust, consistency) | S |
| 8 | **Share a live-tracking link and mask calls.** At minimum add the public trip link to the share text. Plan a masked-calling provider. | High (safety and privacy) | M |
| 9 | **One sheet/menu language and menu position.** Glass bottom sheets for fare details, safety and cancel; 12 px radius on the popup; the menu button in the same corner on every screen. Fix the heart vs star for saved places, and colour-by-meaning in the driver icons. | Medium (the "same app everywhere" feel) | M |
| 10 | **Start i18n now.** Set up ARB files, `flutter_localizations` and string extraction, then Hindi and Marathi. It gets more expensive with every screen added. | High for reach | L |

*Next after the top 10:* smaller APK (App Bundle, remove unshipped theme art), receipt as a proper invoice, offer card mini-map, driver name and photo at onboarding, iOS rebuild on the Mac.
