# Promo banner photography

Photos used as `PromoBanner` backgrounds on the rider Home (`kMockPromos`).
All are from Unsplash and each photo page states "Free to use under the
Unsplash License" (checked 2026-09-25; none is an Unsplash+ photo). The
Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license) allows free commercial use,
modification and redistribution in an app without attribution; credit is
kept here as a courtesy. It grants no rights in trademarks or likenesses
shown, so photos were chosen with no legible brand logos or number plates.

Processing: cropped to 16:10, resized to 1200x750, JPEG q82 (progressive).
